## Engineering Principles

I try to build systems that are:

- **Grounded** — important answers should be traceable to evidence.
- **Permissioned** — the model does not get to decide its own authority.
- **Testable** — representative AI behavior belongs in regression suites.
- **Idempotent** — retries should not create duplicate business actions.
- **Observable** — operators need to understand what happened and why.
- **Secure by design** — secrets, authentication, logging, and least privilege matter.
- **Operationally useful** — the goal is measurable business value, not a flashy demo.
